//Vole Machine Sample program 1
;Vole Machine Program
;Purpose:
;Load two numbers (8 and 9), and perform bitwise OR
;Display the result (9) on the screen
;--------------------------------------------
;1. Load two numbers (8 and 9)
2008;Load hexadecimal 08 into register 0
2109;Load hexadecimal 09 into register 1
;2. OR the two numbers
7201;OR contents of register 0 and register 1 and put result into register 2
;3. Store the result in memory [FF] (screen output)
32FF;Store register 2 into memory [FF] (screen output)
;4. Halt
C000;Halt program execution
